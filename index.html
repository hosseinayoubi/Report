Practical use

I would use the model for low-risk support, not automatic decisions.

  • Prioritize reminders.
  • Offer easier rescheduling.
  • Keep a person in the loop.
  • Monitor subgroup performance.
  • Protect patient data.
Do not use a no-show score to cancel an appointment or reduce access to care.
Source: Medical Appointment No Show dataset (Kaggle). Analysis uses a 70/30 group split by PatientId. No-show is the positive class.
